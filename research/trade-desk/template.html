Best legal lineup under the chosen measure. Bench = the next three best. Tap a column to sort, a team for its lineup and full roster.
Pts/wk = games ÷ (games + 7) × recent form, plus the rest from the market price turned into points. FC = FantasyCalc half-PPR trade value. Lineup is QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, TE, 2 FLEX. Kickers and defences are left out because a trade doesn't change them.
Chance they accept is a rough guide, not a model: it weighs what the deal is worth to them (FantasyCalc value, with a QB they wouldn't start counting a fifth, since he only covers byes and injuries) equally with what it does to their lineup: 10% of value balances 1.5 points a week. Then it drops for each player they'd have to cut and each injured player they'd take on.
Other teams keep their current starters unless a new player is at least 2 points a week better, so a backup they'd never start adds nothing for them. In a 3-way, tap a player again to change who he goes to.
Projection = Sleeper's next-week projection, with its usual overstatement by position taken off. Empty slots are filled with the best free agent's Pts/wk under every measure.
Each extra point a week is worth about 1.6% more chance of winning that week. FantasyCalc pays extra for the best player in a deal, so on a 3-for-1 aim well above 1.15×.
